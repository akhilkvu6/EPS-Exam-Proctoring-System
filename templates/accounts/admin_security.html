Hardening Overview

EPS operates under strict institutional security measures:

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Explicit view decorators guarantee only authenticated students, teachers, or administrators can access authorized views.
Verification Gate: Unverified accounts cannot participate in exams or access test authoring tools until formal faculty or administrator approval.
Departmental Boundaries: Faculty approvals and exam creation are strictly isolated by department to prevent cross-department privilege escalation.
Session & CSRF Security: Encrypted session tokens and CSRF token protection on all state-altering requests protect against cross-site attacks.
